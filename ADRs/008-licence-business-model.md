# ADR-008 — Licence and business model

**Status:** Proposed — the deliberately-missing ADR, now named. Closes before
any public release.

## Context
jsreport's 5-template ceiling is both the market gap and the cautionary tale;
Carbone's CCL restricts hosted-DGaaS. A solo vendor needs a line that funds
the work without killing adoption.

## Candidate line (the Sidekiq rule)
Everything one company needs to run its own documents: free, forever, OSI
licence. Paid: what operators-of-many and compliance departments need —
multi-tenant, SSO/SCIM, shadow-parity dashboard, signed audit exports,
retention policy UI, support SLA. Self-hosted-first is the trust posture
(no DPA, source as escrow).

## Open
Exact OSI licence for the core; whether render-carbone's CCL dependency
constrains distribution; trademark on the name.
